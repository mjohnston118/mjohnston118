## Public Engagement
I'm committed to public engagement and knowledge exchange, sharing developmental science with families, teachers, and fellow researchers.

During my postdoc, I obtained a KEI grant to host a "Cognitive Science Discovery Day" — a drop-in event for families to learn about developmental science methods, including distributing leaflets explaining cognitive control and mental health resilience techniques for children (see flyer below). 


I've also presented at public engagement events including a Healthy Ageing Showcase at Heriot-Watt University (2024), and explained experimental design for the public-facing "Meet the Scientist" seminar series by Gorilla Experiment Builder (see video below).

During my PhD, I organised student-led events including a PhD student-led conference and "Psych Hike" — a two-day wellbeing and professional development event for postgraduate researchers, funded by PsyPAG, combining presentation skills training, careers panels, a mental health/work-life balance session, and a hike promoting student wellbeing.
