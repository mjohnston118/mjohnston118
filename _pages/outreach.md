I'm committed to public engagement and knowledge exchange, sharing developmental science with families, teachers, and fellow researchers.

During my postdoc, I obtained a KEI grant to host a "Cognitive Science Discovery Day" — a drop-in event for families to learn about developmental science methods, including distributing leaflets explaining cognitive control and mental health resilience techniques for children. 

<img src="/assets/img/Wee_Science_Day_Flyer.jpg" alt="Wee Science Day flyer" style="max-width: 400px; width: 100%;">

I've also presented at public engagement events including a Healthy Ageing Showcase at Heriot-Watt University (2024), and explained experimental design for the public-facing "Meet the Scientist" seminar series by Gorilla Experiment Builder ([watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UhAl90DgaXM)).

During my PhD, I organised student-led events including a PhD student-led conference and "Psych Hike" — a two-day wellbeing and professional development event for postgraduate researchers, funded by PsyPAG, combining presentation skills training, careers panels, a mental health/work-life balance session, and a hike promoting student wellbeing.
